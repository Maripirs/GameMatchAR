How GameMatch Works

Choose your player count, time, and complexity filters, then scan a shelf or upload a photo.

GameMatch freezes the image, finds board-game boxes, matches each crop against your local reference database, and shows game details only when the match is confident.

Tap Yes or No on a match to help tune future results. Swipe a match card sideways to dismiss it.
