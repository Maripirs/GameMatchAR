How GameMatch Works

Set players, time and complexity at the top, then scan a shelf, upload a photo, or try an example.

Advanced adds theme, mechanic, age, language, rating, rank and more. Theme and mechanic take several values at once and a game needs any one of them.

Answers appear on the photo: games that fit are highlighted and named, ones that do not are outlined in grey, and a dashed outline means the match was uncertain.

Tap a box for its card, or a card to flash its box. Each card marks players, time and complexity per filter, so you can see what a game missed on.

Each card links straight to BoardGameGeek. See more opens full details and reporting a wrong match, and Missing a game? lets you draw a box the scanner skipped.
